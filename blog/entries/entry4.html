Stupid ass rant

I think we should really separate "beats" from "songs" because I feel like a lot of people mix the two, meanwhile they are different. A "beat" is a piece of music someone sings lyrics along. Listening to it by itself will not invoke the emotion the lyrics were trying to get you to feel. A "song" is a full blown composition. It tries to invoke a mood, to make you feel something. For example, a song could be sad, a song could be happy, a song could be melancholic, etc. Most importantly, it has a melody, it has something to give you. I am not saying that songs should not have lyrics, they can. What I'm saying is we need to separate songs with a melody and a emotion they're trying to invoke from beats that a rapper sings to. Also, it really puts me off in a way to see people looking at producers and thinking it's all trap beats and whatnot, because it isn't. People have different tastes. (P.
